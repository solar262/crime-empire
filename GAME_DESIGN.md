# Crime Empire Game Design Rules

## Product Direction
Crime Empire is a mobile-first browser strategy game. It should feel like an idle tycoon plus territory war game.

The core loop is:

1. Collect cash
2. Buy businesses for income
3. Hire crew for power
4. Use energy on jobs, bosses, and factions
5. Take districts on the city map
6. Manage heat and pressure
7. Upgrade HQ to improve long-term strategy

## Main Resources

### Cash
Used to buy businesses, crew, HQ upgrades, and districts.
Cash should increase steadily, not jump wildly.

### Crew Power
Determines what the player can challenge.
Power comes from:
- crew members
- respect
- controlled districts
- HQ Training
- Auto Garage
- Crown Tower

### Energy
Limits active play.
Energy refills slowly over time.
Energy is spent on:
- jobs
- boss attacks
- faction moves

### Heat / Pressure
Represents city attention and instability.
High pressure creates penalties.
Players manage it through:
- Lay Low
- Legal Team
- Private Security
- Media Company

### Respect
Long-term status resource.
Respect helps power and rank.

## City Map Rules
Districts must be clear and functional.
Each district needs:
- cash requirement
- power requirement
- clear locked reason
- reward/benefit shown

Taking a district:
- costs cash
- requires enough power
- increases respect
- increases heat/pressure
- adds income bonus

A district button should never be confusing. If locked, it must say why.

## Business Rules
Businesses should have different strategic roles.

- Corner Shop: safe income
- Night Club: higher income, higher pressure
- Auto Garage: power support
- Private Security: pressure control
- Import Warehouse: big income, higher pressure
- Luxury Hotel: respect over time
- Media Company: pressure control
- Crown Tower: endgame income and power boost

## Mission Rules
Mission progress should not go backwards because the player spends cash.
Mission bars should use best progress or lifetime progress.

## Balance Rules
Avoid extreme jumps.
Reward chests and daily rewards should be capped.
Pressure penalties should hurt but not destroy progress.

## Development Rules
Before adding a feature:
1. Define what player problem it solves
2. Define the resource cost
3. Define the reward
4. Define the failure/lock condition
5. Show the rule clearly in the UI
6. Test on mobile
